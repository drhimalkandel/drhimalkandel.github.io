Dr Himal Kandel

Kornhauser Research Fellow
Save Sight Institute · Faculty of Medicine and Health · The University of Sydney

I am a vision and outcomes researcher with research interests spanning patient-reported outcomes, quality of life, corneal disease, keratoconus, refractive error and clinical registries. My work focuses on understanding patient outcomes and generating evidence that can improve eye care, clinical decision-making and patient-centred practice.

Research Focus

Outcomes Research

Evaluation of clinical and patient-reported outcomes using longitudinal data, statistical modelling and modern outcome measurement approaches.

Quality of Life

Development and evaluation of patient-reported outcome measures, including psychometric methods such as Rasch analysis and item-response theory.

Cornea & Keratoconus

Real-world research into keratoconus, corneal cross-linking, disease progression, treatment outcomes and the patient impact of corneal disease.

Refractive Error

Research examining the impact of refractive error and its correction on people's quality of life, including development of refractive error-specific item banks.

About

I am based at the Save Sight Institute, The University of Sydney, where my research includes clinical registry studies, patient-reported outcomes and corneal research. My work involves multidisciplinary and international collaborations, research supervision, statistical analysis, grant development and translation of research findings into clinically relevant evidence.
