Media & Engagement

My research has been featured in professional and public-facing media, particularly in relation to dry eye disease, keratoconus, corneal cross-linking, quality of life and patient-reported outcomes.

I also contribute to research translation through podcasts, community events, webinars, professional education and engagement with patients, clinicians and the wider community.

Featured Podcast

RESEARCH BYTES

Dry Eye Disease with Dr Himal Kandel

I was invited by the Dean of the Faculty of Medicine and Health at The University of Sydney to feature on the Research Bytes podcast and discuss my research into the impact of dry eye disease on patients' quality of life.

The conversation explores the clinical and patient-centred implications of dry eye disease and highlights the importance of incorporating patients' perspectives when evaluating outcomes of eye care.
