Leadership & Service

My academic leadership spans research governance, clinical registries, researcher development, professional organisations and service to the broader vision science community.

I have particular interests in supporting early- and mid-career researchers, strengthening research systems and contributing to collaborative academic and professional initiatives.

University & Researcher Leadership

Sydney Early and Mid Career Academic Network

I serve as Executive Treasurer and Faculty of Medicine and Health representative for the Sydney Early and Mid Career Academic Network (SEMCAN) at The University of Sydney.

Through SEMCAN, I contribute to university-wide initiatives supporting mentoring, researcher development and engagement of early- and mid-career academics.

Save Sight Institute Early Career Researchers Committee Foundation Co-chair.

Research Governance

I contribute to research governance through the Save Sight Keratoconus Registry, with roles supporting the development and oversight of registry-based research.

Save Sight Keratoconus Registry Advisory Committee Secretary and committee member.
Save Sight Keratoconus Registry Steering Committee Secretary and committee member.

Professional & International Service

ARVO

Served on the Association for Research in Vision and Ophthalmology Global Members Committee and have contributed to international researcher-development activities.

Vision 2020 Australia

Served as a member of the Vision 2020 Australia Global Committee.

International Research Taskforces

Contributed to collaborative international initiatives including the International Myopia Institute and British Contact Lens Association CLEAR taskforces.

Research Community Service

Contribute to research conferences, fellowship assessment, academic review and professional activities within ophthalmology and vision science.

Researcher Development & Mentoring

Supporting researchers and strengthening mentoring culture are important components of my academic service.

The Good Mentor Programme

Contributed to the organisation and delivery of The Good Mentor initiative through SEMCAN and The University of Sydney research community.

Career Development

Invited as an expert panellist for the ARVO Advance Conference Session with an Expert: Career Paths, contributing to career guidance and mentoring for early-career researchers.

I contribute to Higher Degree Research governance at the Faculty of Medicine and Health, The University of Sydney, including chairing Progress Evaluation Meeting panels and providing independent assessment of candidature progress.

Scholarly Service

Journal Peer Review Extensive peer-review activity across ophthalmology, optometry and vision-science journals.
Research Assessment Service as a judge for research conferences, awards and fellowship programmes.
Higher Degree Research Examination Contribution to independent assessment of doctoral research and academic standards.

Leadership Approach

My approach to academic leadership is collaborative and focused on strengthening research environments, supporting people and contributing to high-quality research governance.
